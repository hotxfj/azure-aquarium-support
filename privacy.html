Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

In the aquarium

The aquarium runs on your device. The developer does not collect personal data through the app. The app has no account system, advertising or third-party analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Preferences and animation

Your device stores exhibit settings such as the clock, lighting and graphics quality. The app measures elapsed time locally to animate the exhibit.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. The app uses Apple's verified transaction information on your device to unlock features. The developer does not receive your payment details through the app. Apple’s privacy policy explains how Apple handles its services.

Visiting this site and contacting support

GitHub hosts this site and processes connection information when you visit. If you choose to post a support request, GitHub and the developer can see what you submit, and the request is public. Please leave out personal information. GitHub’s privacy statement describes its practices.

For questions about this policy, visit Azure Aquarium support.
