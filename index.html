Aquarium support

Azure Aquarium is a virtual whale shark exhibit for iPhone and iPad.

What does Full Aquarium unlock?

One purchase unlocks the full schools, night lighting, luminous animals and the underwater follow camera. Future species updates are included.

How do I restore my purchase?

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. In the exhibit, open Settings (•••) and choose Restore Purchases. If the store is unavailable, check your connection and try again.

How can I get smoother motion?

Open Settings and turn on Low graphics. You can switch it off at any time.

Get in touch

Send a support request. Requests are public. Include your device model and app version, and leave out account details, receipts and other personal information. For billing or refunds, contact Apple.
